How I Work

The Editing Workflow

A streamlined, collaborative editing process built to take the headache out of post-production and ensure premium quality.

01

Footage Ingest & Sort

Send me your raw files (via Drive/Frame.io). I organize bins, sync multi-cam tracks, align external audio, and log the best takes.

02

The Storyboard / Rough Cut

I assemble the narrative, pacing, and basic flow. This cut ensures the story is airtight before adding complex visuals.

03

VFX & Sound Design

I add custom motion graphics, sound effects (SFX), transitions, audio enhancement, and ambient noise to bring the scene alive.

04

Color Grading

Using professional color tools, I correct exposure, match cameras, and apply a creative grade matching the emotional tone.

05

Revision & Delivery

We review the video on Frame.io. After applying your feedback, I deliver high-quality optimized exports for your target platforms.
